## Observed again

**Ulysses — opening and scale.** The early screenshot shows a centered compact black mark on a white field. The settled screenshot shows a huge black graphic SVG mark occupying most of the lower viewport, compact navigation at the top, a small three-line statement beneath the mark, and the first project images at the bottom. PageDown reveals unequal square-edged photographic panels, thin gutters, substantial unused white space and a small project label. This corroborates the earlier opening-to-gallery sequence. The sampled waits are not measured animation durations or evidence of a particular loading strategy.

**Vodjo — agency information flow.** The initial screenshot exposes the software-house proposition, supporting paragraph, outlined capsule contact action and laptop photograph immediately. Header navigation includes agency, services, portfolio, blog, careers and contact. PageDown exposes a white sticky header, dark-to-white wave transition and two-column portfolio imagery. The DOM heading order remains hero → portfolio → services → clients → consultation. DOM requests Poppins 300/400/600. These are observed content and style declarations; exact computed typography, pointer hover and lead delivery were not measured.

**Until — type, buttons and scrolling.** The desktop screenshot shows a large cream sans-serif sentence with very tight spacing near the bottom of a full-viewport photographic particle-like scene. A translucent rounded navigation capsule contains compact navigation and a cream filled contact/recruiting action. After PageDown, the camera-like crop moves closer, the navigation contracts to mark plus CTA, and the hero sentence becomes faint. The mobile normal-motion sample shows a narrow crop, mark/CTA/menu navigation and a readable two-line heading near the lower edge. This differs from the earlier mobile/reduced-motion sample in which the desktop heading was unreadable; the refreshed sample supports a normal-motion narrow layout only. It does not establish the cause of the earlier result or prove the reduced-motion fallback is correct.

Until DOM declares display headings with `leading-[0.95]`, medium weight and negative tracking. The visible hero sentence has a full readable `aria-label`. Mobile navigation uses a semantic button with an accessible name, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and focus-visible styling. A menu CTA declares filled dark/light hover colors, rounded geometry and motion-reduction transition classes. These declarations do not prove actual hover timing, keyboard focus management or Escape handling. The exact font family remains unverified.

## Design implications for the current Wasla revision

These are original implementation recommendations, not claims about the references' source code or measured values.

1. Give the opening one recognizable Wasla gesture: a large navy wordmark or original connection composition, with the service proposition and consultation action readable immediately. An entrance can be brief and nonblocking; do not make the page depend on a splash or several scrolls.
2. Use Vodjo's clarity of information order: proposition → selected work → services → collaboration/process → consultation. Keep real detail destinations and explicit project context. Avoid copying its wave separator, imagery, client claims or compact desktop chrome.
3. Translate Until's hierarchy into navy and white: tight, deliberate display wraps; considerably smaller supporting copy; one consistent family of filled, outline and text actions. Use native controls with visible focus and comfortable touch areas. Exact reference font names and dimensions are unnecessary to achieve the hierarchy.
4. Treat Ulysses' asymmetric gallery as a pacing principle: vary work image scale and spacing where the content supports it. Avoid repeating its proprietary mark, precise offsets or mark-to-gallery composition.
5. Preserve full text opacity and static decorative composition for reduced motion. Check 390px separately under both motion preferences, because earlier captures coupled width and preference. Keep a reliable mobile menu and contact entry while scrolling.
