# Wasla — revised visual direction

Status: approved user direction, code-first. Supersedes the previous homepage composition.

The user explicitly chose the familiar agency path alongside Ulysses de Santi, Vodjo, and Until Labs. That pinned direction takes precedence over the concept roll (`seed:f29e8fcfc0cc4447`). No new selection round is needed.

## Direction contract

Mode: Persuade on the homepage; Experience for portfolio discovery; Read for articles; Operate for contact and CMS.

Wasla connects business needs to usable digital products. The visitor is an Indonesian business owner comparing agencies and looking for a clear starting point. The page must demonstrate design attention through the work itself, then explain services and invite a conversation.

Use deep navy and white, large self-hosted Manrope typography, clean rounded rectangular buttons, generous editorial spacing, and high-quality original concept project photographs. Replace the geometric hero placeholder with a large CMS-managed project image. The first screen balances oversized typography and a tangible digital product. Portfolio follows immediately; services are clear linked rows, not a grid of interchangeable icon cards.

Opening motion is a short image reveal with the heading already readable, followed by bounded image parallax on wide screens. Mobile and reduced-motion views are static. There is no blocking loader, artificial wait, scroll hijacking, or hidden content without JavaScript.

Homepage sequence: hero → selected work → services → agency approach → process → optional real team/testimonials → insights → FAQ → consultation → footer. Empty proof sections are omitted. All sample projects remain labelled concepts. No copied client names, testimonials, contact details, source code, or reference artwork.

Reference influence: Ulysses supplies scale, image pacing and negative space; Vodjo supplies clear agency hierarchy and early project discovery; Until supplies compact navigation, confident sans typography and calm button states. This is an original composition.

## Image policy

Three original generated concept mockups are supplied as replaceable Media records: Ruang commerce, Alur operations, Sela journal. They are examples, not delivered client projects. Their exact prompts and origins are recorded in docs/assets.md and asset metadata. CMS thumbnail fields control portfolio visuals; global heroImage controls the opening. Existing user-selected media is preserved by the asset seeder.
